Custom Order

Have something special in mind? Tell us your idea and we will create it for you.

1

Share Your Idea

Message us the occasion, your preferred flowers and colors, and your budget. You are welcome to send a reference photo of the bouquet you have in mind.

2

We Design It

We suggest a bouquet that fits your idea and budget, and confirm the price with you.

3

Confirm the Details

Approve the design, your card message and the delivery details, and we will guide you through payment.

4

We Make and Deliver

Your bouquet is handcrafted by our florist and delivered to you or your recipient. We will confirm the timing for your design.

What You Can Customize

  • Preserved or dried flowers in your preferred colors
  • Fresh flowers: we can create fresh flower bouquets too
  • Extras: Ferrero chocolates or a money bouquet
  • Size and budget: from small bouquets to large arrangements
  • Reference photo: feel free to send us any bouquet you like as inspiration
  • A free personalized card with your own message
